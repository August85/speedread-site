SpeedRead Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026 · Support

SpeedRead is a reading app for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: SpeedRead does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your iPhone

Documents you import, the text extracted from them, your reading positions, favorites, settings and reading statistics are saved only on your device, inside SpeedRead's own storage. They are included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) under your control, like any other app's data. Deleting a document or the app removes them.

What we collect

Nothing. SpeedRead has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server of its own. We cannot see what you read.

Network requests

SpeedRead connects to the internet only when you import a link. It then downloads that page or file directly from the website you chose. For Wikipedia links, it requests the article's text from Wikipedia's public API. Those websites receive the same basic information any web request includes, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Children

SpeedRead does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or support requests: open a support request.
