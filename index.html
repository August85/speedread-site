SpeedRead

Read faster, one word at a time. An iPhone app by Abhimanyu Katyayan.

Get help

Found a bug, a page that won't import, or have an idea? Open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed) and we'll reply there.

Common questions

How do I read an article from Safari or Chrome?
Copy the page's link, open SpeedRead and tap Paste Link on Home. You can also tap + in Library and choose From a Link.

Why won't a page import?
SpeedRead downloads the page itself, so pages behind a login or paywall, and sites that build their text with scripts, can't be imported yet. Public articles, blogs and Wikipedia work.

Why won't a PDF import?
Scanned PDFs are pictures of pages with no text inside. Support for those is planned.

Where is my data?
Only on your iPhone. SpeedRead has no account and collects nothing. See the privacy policy.
